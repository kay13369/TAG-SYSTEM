About TAG

Botswana-built, future-focused ICT

Technology Affiliates Group (TAG) is a 100% citizen-owned technology services company based in Gaborone, established in 2014. With over 8 years of experience, we deliver innovative, scalable, and secure ICT solutions that empower businesses to thrive in a digital-first world — helping organisations strategically leverage ICT investments while designing robust IT policies that mitigate risk, disaster, and information loss.
