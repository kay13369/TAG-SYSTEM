Who we are

A 100% citizen-owned technology partner

Technology Affiliates Group (TAG) is a 100% citizen-owned technology services company based in Gaborone, Botswana, established in 2014. With over 8 years of experience, we deliver innovative, scalable, and secure ICT solutions that empower businesses to thrive in a digital-first world.

We help organisations strategically leverage their ICT investments while designing robust IT policies that mitigate risk, disaster, and information loss — becoming the preferred creator of business environments where ICT is at the helm of every process.
